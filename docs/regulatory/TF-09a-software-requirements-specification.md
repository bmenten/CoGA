## 1. Conventions

- **ID scheme:** `REQ-<AREA>-NNN`. Areas: NIPT, CARR, PGT, DIAG, CLASS, TRACE, SEC, DATA, QC, MITO, CNIPT, PERF, UI, RPT.
- **Criticality** (per requirement): **C** = a failure could contribute to a wrong clinical result; **B** = could mislead but is normally caught; **A** = no injury possible. This is not an IEC 62304 software safety class: the software as a whole is Class C, with no lower-class decomposition ([TF-07 §1](TF-07-software-lifecycle-plan.md)).
- **Risk** column: the hazard IDs in the [Risk Management Plan (TF-06 §6)](TF-06-risk-management-plan.md); "—" means no hazard is linked yet ([TF-09 §3](TF-09-verification-validation.md)).
- Every requirement is **verifiable**; the RTM holds the verifying evidence.
  [TF-09b §3](TF-09b-requirements-traceability-matrix.md) lists the requirements with weak or
  absent verification.

## 2. Scope & assumptions

- Scope is the CoGA device per [TF-02](TF-02-device-description.md): from validated annotated VCF/tracks to the closed, frozen analysis a supervisor signs out. The clinical report is drafted and medically signed in the EPD, outside the device, and refers 1:1 to that analysis ([TF-02 §3](TF-02-device-description.md)).
- **Assumption:** inputs come from separately validated upstream workflows; CoGA does not validate primary calling/annotation (TF-01 §4).
- Functional requirements are grouped by clinical application and cross-cutting module.

| REQ-CNIPT-002 | Draw a plasma's ccnv tracks in the genome and chromosome views on a NIPT axis of their own, each segment in the colour of ccnv's call and a call ccnv flags discordant apart, with lines at log2(1 ± FF/2), where a fetal trisomy and monosomy lie at the plasma's effective fetal fraction, on chromosomes 1–22 and X, never on Y; draw none without a fetal fraction, and say so. | REQ-CNIPT-004 | Make a finding of each segment ccnv called and each whose \|z-score\| reaches the grey-zone bound: an aneuploidy of its chromosome where the findings of its direction (gain or loss) on that chromosome span together at least half of the chromosome's segmented length, one segment or several as ccnv cut it, otherwise a segmental CNV. ## 5. Maintenance

This SRS is revised whenever a change adds or alters a requirement (TF-18). A new requirement
takes the next free ID in its area. From the first release candidate, when change control
applies ([TF-18 §3a](TF-18-change-configuration-management.md)), a retired requirement is marked
deprecated, not deleted, to keep its traceability.
