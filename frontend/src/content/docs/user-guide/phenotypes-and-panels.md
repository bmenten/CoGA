Phenotype-led analysis is central to modern interpretation. Capture the patient's clinical features
as HPO terms and curate gene sets so the same prioritisation can be reused across cases.

### HPO phenotypes

- Browse and search the HPO ontology from the HPO page.
- Annotate individuals with HPO terms in the family workspace; phenotype annotations are stored per
  individual and travel with the family.
- Use the phenotype to decide which genes and inheritance models to prioritise.

### Gene panels

- The gene panel catalog holds reusable gene lists for recurring indications.
- Apply a panel directly in the small-variant or structural-variant filter to restrict a search to a
  curated set of genes — a fast way to focus a broad genome on the clinically relevant loci.
- Panels can be imported automatically from PanelApp or custom-made, and are managed centrally so a
  team shares the same definitions.
