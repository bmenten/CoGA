- **ACMG class** — the five-tier variant classification: benign, likely benign, VUS, likely pathogenic,
  pathogenic.
- **Affected versus carrier** — affected status describes the person, carrier status the genotype. They
  are recorded separately: an unaffected person can be a carrier.
- **APCAD** — *Definition to be confirmed by the laboratory.* On screen, the APCAD track shows
  parent-of-origin markers (0 to 1) for a phased trio, or the folded minor allele fraction (0 to 0.5)
  from a depth-based caller.
- **Assembly** — the reference genome build (for example GRCh38) a project uses.
- **cfDNA** — cell-free DNA. In NIPT: the DNA in maternal plasma, mostly maternal with a small fetal part.
- **ClinVar** — the public archive of variant–condition interpretations.
- **CNV / SV** — a copy-number variant is a deletion or duplication; a structural variant is any larger
  rearrangement, including CNVs, insertions, inversions and translocations.
- **Compound heterozygous** — two different variants in one gene, one on each copy (in trans).
- **De novo** — present in a child and absent in both parents. On a son's X or Y outside the
  pseudo-autosomal regions, only the mother (X) or the father (Y) passes that chromosome on.
- **Embryo call** — CoGA's derived PGT result for an embryo at the ROI: affected / at risk, carrier,
  unaffected or uninformative.
- **Fetal fraction (FF)** — the share of the cfDNA that comes from the fetus.
- **gnomAD / TOPMed** — population allele-frequency references.
- **Haplotype, homolog** — a homolog is one of a person's two copies of a chromosome; a haplotype is the
  set of alleles on one homolog.
- **Heteroplasmy** — the fraction of mitochondrial genomes that carry a variant.
- **HPO** — the Human Phenotype Ontology: a hierarchy of clinical phenotype terms, from general to
  specific.
- **IBD** — identity by descent: a stretch of DNA that two relatives inherited from the same ancestor.
- **IBS0** — the share of sites where two samples have opposite homozygous genotypes; near zero for a
  parent and child.
- **In-silico predictors** — CADD, REVEL, SpliceAI, SIFT, PolyPhen and AlphaMissense: scores that
  predict a variant's effect or splice impact.
- **Kinship (φ)** — a measure of relatedness: about 0.25 for parent and child or siblings, 0.125 for
  second-degree relatives.
- **LoF** — loss of function: a variant predicted to abolish the gene product (stop gained, frameshift,
  canonical splice site).
- **MANE Select / MANE Plus Clinical** — agreed reference transcripts for clinical reporting; **RefSeq
  Select** and **Ensembl Canonical** are each database's representative transcript.
- **Mendelian error** — a child's genotype that cannot come from its parents' genotypes; many of them
  point to a sample swap or a wrong pedigree.
- **Mendeliome** — CoGA's built-in panel of every gene Monarch links to a disease; the default scope of
  the variant pages.
- **MNV** — multi-nucleotide variant: neighbouring bases changed together.
- **Monarch Initiative** — a knowledge graph that links genes, diseases and HPO phenotypes.
- **NeedLR** — the structural-variant annotation tool of the long-read pipeline; its annotations fill
  the *Needlr Annotations* filters.
- **P / LP / B / LB** — pathogenic, likely pathogenic, benign, likely benign.
- **Paraphase** — a caller for genes in paralogous or duplicated regions.
- **PGT** — preimplantation genetic testing: testing embryos before transfer.
- **Phenotype similarity** — a score for how well two sets of HPO terms match, weighting rare, specific
  terms more heavily.
- **pLI / LOEUF** — gene constraint: how intolerant a gene is to loss of function (a high pLI or a low
  LOEUF means constrained).
- **popmax** — the highest allele frequency in any gnomAD sub-population.
- **Priority score** — the Exomiser-style score that ranks candidates within a family; not a probability.
- **Recombination** — a crossover between the two homologs at meiosis; the haplotype an embryo carries
  changes at that point.
- **ROI** — region of interest: the gene or locus a family is about.
- **Trans / cis** — two variants on opposite homologs (trans) or on the same homolog (cis).
- **TRGT** — tandem-repeat genotyping, used for repeat-expansion calls.
- **VAF** — variant allele fraction: the share of reads that carry the alternate allele.
- **VUS tier (hot / warm / cold)** — a split of the VUS band by ACMG points: hot 4–5, warm 2–3, cold 0–1.
