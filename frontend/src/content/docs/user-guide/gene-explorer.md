When the question is about a gene rather than a single case, the Gene Explorer gives a consolidated
profile, similar to the gene pages in dedicated interpretation platforms.

### Transcript overview

The transcript table badges the clinically relevant transcripts so you can pick the right one at a
glance:

- **MANE Select** — the agreed default clinical transcript.
- **MANE Plus Clinical** — additional transcripts needed for clinical reporting.
- **RefSeq Select** — the representative RefSeq transcript.
- **Ensembl Canonical** — the Ensembl canonical transcript.

### Gene-level context

- Constraint metrics (e.g. pLI, LOEUF, missense constraint) for dosage sensitivity.
- Disease and phenotype associations, ClinGen / GenCC evidence, and OMIM links.
- **Monarch gene–disease associations** and each disease’s expected HPO phenotypes, with patient
  overlap when opened in a family context — see [Phenotype matching](#phenotype-matching).
- External links out to the major knowledge resources for the gene.
